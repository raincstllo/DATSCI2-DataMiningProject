Revenue and services

Forecasts the next months' revenue and service demand with a Random Forest, and finds which services tend to go together for the same patient with Apriori. Use it to plan staffing, supplies and service packages.

Services that go together

Lift above 1 means the pair happens more often than chance; the higher, the stronger. Use strong pairs for package offers or follow-up reminders.
