Patient retention

Groups patients into segments by how recently they visited, how often they come and how much they have spent, using K-Means clustering. Use the segments to plan follow-ups.

Run analysis

Loads the visit records, cleans them with PySpark, then runs K-Means. Takes 1 to 2 minutes.
